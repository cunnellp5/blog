hi im phil.

I was about 7 years old when I enrobed Oreos on a chocolate conveyor belt, earning free desserts and a 20-dollar bill which I had zero concept of what to do with other than put it in a ceramic pig.

Fast forward to graduating college, and I found myself working as a "mud-logger" on the ol' oil patch for an oil company, stationed in North Dakota and spending about two months in Alaska.

After layoffs driven by low gas prices, I found myself delivering beer across the greater Denver area.

While lifting kegs in and out of a box truck was thrilling, I eventually went to Galvanize, where I began my life as a software developer between 2016 and 2017.

After six months of full-stack learning, I was lucky enough to solve a riddle about jelly beans which landed me a job at a customer journey analytics company called ClickFox.

Since my early days working with Angular 2+ at ClickFox, I've gathered skills and languages spanning the full stack. Python, Go, React, Vue, and Node to name the main ones. More details can be found in the [resume](https://resume.philcunnell.dev/)
